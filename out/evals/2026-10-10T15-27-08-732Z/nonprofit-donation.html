Scientific Framework

Planting Resilient Forests, Not Fuel For The Next Fire

Historic post-fire replanting packed monoculture trees too close together, creating dense brush fires decades later. We use climate-smart silviculture.

PHASE 01

Soil & Ash Chemistry

Before planting, we test hydrophobicity (water-repelling glass crusts formed by fierce heat) and inoculate soils with native mycorrhizal fungi.

Timeline: Months 1–4 Post-Fire
PHASE 02

Zone-Matched Seed Banking

We pull seeds from our partner conservancy vaults, matching elevation zones to 50-year forward climate projections rather than past averages.

Elevation: 3,200 – 7,800 ft
PHASE 03

Cluster-Mosaic Planting

Hand-planting in irregular clumps with varied spacing mimicking old-growth fire-resilient patterns, leaving strategic bare fuel breaks.

Technique: Variable Density
PHASE 04

Datalogging & Survivorship

Each planting plot receives sub-meter GPS tagging. Technicians revisit at 12, 24, and 36 months to replace drought-stressed losses.

Assurance: 36 Months Active Guard
