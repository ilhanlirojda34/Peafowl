Our Heritage

Rooted in Kayseri, cooked with patience in London.

The 26-Year-Old Starter

When Mehmet and Zehra Kaya left Central Anatolia in the late nineties, they carried a small crock of fermented dough starter wrapped in wool. That same sourdough culture gives our Tırnak Pidesi its smoky blisters and airy crumb today.

Flour • Water • Sea Salt • Time

The Hand-Built Stone Oven

Our dining room is anchored by a custom firebrick oven fueled exclusively by seasoned English oak. Baking at 450°C sears meats in seconds to lock in natural juices while imparting the unmistakable scent of the steppe.

Seasoned British Oak • 450°C

Spices with Lineage

We work directly with cousin growers in Gaziantep, Maraş, and Urfa. Sun-dried isot pepper cured in olive oil, wild mountain thyme (kekik), and hand-picked sumac berries arrive seasonally without industrial additives.

Misafirperverlik • Sacred Hospitality

In Anatolia, no traveler leaves a table hungry.

The Turkish word Diyar translates to “homeland” or “realm”. When we built our oven on Albion Parade twenty-six years ago, we brought more than recipes: we brought the courtyard ritual of long, unhurried dinners where platters are passed continuously and fresh tea arrives unprompted.

  • Complimentary Warm Lavash & Za'atar Dip on arrival for every table
  • Pure Wood Charcoal: Zero gas grills or electric ovens touch our kebabs
  • Always Finished with Çay: Fresh-brewed black tea from Rize on the house
