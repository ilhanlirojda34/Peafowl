How We Practice

Simple hospitality on and off the mat

We intentionally keep our studio small so teachers can learn your name, your modifications, and what brings you through the door.

No Performance

No mirrors on our practice walls. We encourage moving by internal sensation rather than aesthetic appearance or competition.

All Props Ready

Clean Manduka cork mats, organic buckwheat bolsters, handcrafted Mexican wool blankets, and wooden blocks are set up for you.

Herbal Tea Table

Arrive 15 minutes before practice to pour a warm mug of local chamomile or roasted barley tea in our shoe-free entryway lounge.

Our Offerings

Four paths to presence

Choose whatever your nervous system is asking for today.

01 • Alignment

Gentle Hatha

Slow, deliberate postures held for several breaths. Excellent for beginners, recovering bodies, or anyone seeking structural balance.

Pacing: Unhurried • Room: 72°F
02 • Flow

Slow Flow Vinyasa

Breath synchronized with movement. Transitions are steady and unhurried with clear modifications offered throughout.

Pacing: Moderate • Room: 76–78°F
03 • Stillness

Yin Yoga

Floor-based passive stretches held for 3 to 5 minutes to gently hydrate connective tissue and calm the parasympathetic nervous system.

Pacing: Still • Room: 73°F
04 • Reset

Sound & Restorative

Completely supported in bolsters and blankets without muscular effort. Accompanied by Tibetan singing bowls and chimes.

Pacing: Passive • Room: 74°F
