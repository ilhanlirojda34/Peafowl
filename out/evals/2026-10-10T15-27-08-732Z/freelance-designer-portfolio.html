Studio Philosophy

Design grounded in physical truth, not fleeting design trends.

Working out of a quiet studio overlooking the Tagus estuary in Graça, I run an intentional, boutique studio practice. Rather than juggling dozens of simultaneous commissions, I partner with a select group of clients each year.

I believe that brands in the modern era crave weight, texture, and silence. Great branding doesn't need to shout over the noise; it speaks through deliberate typographic choice, authentic substrates, tactile embossing, and coherent storytelling across both physical and digital realms.

The Method

A straightforward, four-stage engagement.

Every collaboration follows a structured path with direct communication at every touchpoint. No account managers, no layers.

Phase 01

Discovery & Grounding

In-depth immersion into your story, audience, competitors, and physical reality. We establish tone, parameters, and design pillars.

Timeline: 2 Weeks
Phase 02

Creative Directions

Presentation of two rigorously distinct conceptual territories with real-world applications across packaging, type, and digital mockups.

Timeline: 3 Weeks
Phase 03

Refinement & Craft

Selecting the chosen route and perfecting every millimeter: vector point refinement, color calibrations, and paper sample testing.

Timeline: 2–3 Weeks
Phase 04

Handoff & Production

Delivering master production-ready assets, comprehensive brand guidelines, printer liaisons, and digital guidelines.

Timeline: 1–2 Weeks
