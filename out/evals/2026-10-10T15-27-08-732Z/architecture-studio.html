02 / Studio Philosophy

We build with materials that do not decay into garbage, but weather into dignity.

AXIOM I [MASS]

Thermal Inertia Over Complex Machines

We design thick mineral envelopes with genuine structural weight. Buildings with high thermal storage density handle summer peaks and winter cold snaps naturally, cutting reliance on short-lived mechanical sensors, filters, and digital building management systems.

AXIOM II [LIGHT]

Calibrated Daylight As Geometry

Windows are not arbitrary glass holes; they are optical lenses tuned to geographical solar declination. We design deep window jambs, light scoops, and courtyard shafts so rooms transform throughout the day without blinding glare or heat entrapment.

AXIOM III [RESTRAINT]

Reduction To The Structural Minimum

When a wall carries a load, we refuse to cover it with drywall and vinyl paint. Every element must perform at least two functions: structural support and primary finish. What cannot be defended structurally is omitted from the plan.

“A good house does not seek to shout over the topography. It accepts rain, snow, and sunlight as collaborators, settling into the earth until it feels inevitable.”
— Julian Kern & Elena Voss

03 / The Practice

Small team, complete immersion.

Studio Kern operates deliberately as a compact collective of eight architects, model-makers, and site directors. We limit active construction commissions to four simultaneous sites, ensuring principal involvement from site excavation to the final joinery oiling.

Our workshop maintains an in-house casting laboratory where 1:1 material mockups of aggregate mixes, mortar joints, and charred timber treatments are tested before tender drawings are released.
