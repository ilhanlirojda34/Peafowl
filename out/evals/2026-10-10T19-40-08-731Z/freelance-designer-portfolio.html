01 • Craft & Hospitality

Olaria do Bairro

An artisan stoneware studio and espresso counter situated on the hills of Graça. The founders needed an identity that honored century-old Portuguese clay traditions while maintaining a contemporary, international gallery presence.

The Challenge

Replacing an improvised cafe look with a unified design system fit for wholesale exports across Europe.

The Solution

Custom wordmark, debossed potter's touchmarks, biodegradable tissue wraps, bilingual exhibition catalogues, and brass facade signage.

Brand Strategy Custom Logotype Sustainable Packaging Signage System
02 • Clean Mobility & Ocean Tech

Vanguard Maritime

A Cascais and Azores-based zero-emission sea transit startup deploying electric hydrofoil passenger vessels across Atlantic archipelagos. Built for institutional credibility and nautical authority.

The Challenge

Conveying aerospace-grade battery engineering and marine safety without slipping into generic green tech clichés.

The Solution

Constructed a modular geometric beacon symbol, a strict high-contrast editorial system, fleet vessel liveries, and pilot instrumentation UI guidelines.

03 • Viticulture & Terroir

Mesa & Vinhedo

An organic low-intervention vineyard recovering ancient granite terrace plots in the Dão DOC. Created an understated label architecture and direct-to-consumer print catalog for small allocations across Parisian, Nordic, and Tokyo wine bars.

The Challenge

Standing out in high-end natural wine cellars while retaining the honest austerity of granite-grown Portuguese grapes.

The Solution

A tactile label system using raw cotton stock, blind debossed soil elevations, wax capsule seals, and numbered botanical archive sheets.
